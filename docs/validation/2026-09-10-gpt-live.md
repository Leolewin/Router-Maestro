### Remaining audit-classification issue

Some successful Codex SSE requests are recorded as `client_cancelled/cancelled`
when the client closes immediately after receiving `response.completed`. This
is not treated as proof of a failed model request, nor is the audit status
silently relabeled as completed.

Two additional GPT live probes isolated the behavior:

- Read through stream EOF: exact text and `response.completed` received;
  audit records `explicit_terminal/completed`.
- Close as soon as `response.completed` arrives: the same exact text and completed
  terminal are received, but audit records `client_cancelled/cancelled`.

Five of the seven interactive Codex requests have this audit classification;
the client transcript nevertheless records all five logical turns completed.
The client behavior and requested transport gates pass. The audit's terminal
classification remains a separate known issue, not fixed in this validation run.
